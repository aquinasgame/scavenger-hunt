The Great Library Scavenger Hunt!

Instructions

The Aquinas library has decided to host a scavenger hunt! You can do it on your lunch, or before class starts, or when you're skipping! If you're one of the first to win, you can get a little treat. But before you go jumping for joy, let me explain the rules: Here you will find four clues that each will tell you a specific word. Once you've solved all the clues, say the four-word phrase you discover to Ms. Perera in the library.

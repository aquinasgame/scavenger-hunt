The Great Library Scavenger Hunt!

Instructions

The Aquinas library has decided to host a scavenger hunt. You can do it on your lunch, or before class starts, or when you're skipping! The challenge is simple: Here you will find four clues that each will tell you a specific word. Once you've solved all the clues, say the four-word phrase you discover to Ms. Perera, and you'll win a little treat.

Clue #1

Search it twice, the fifth door on the left, just before eight o'clock, and I'll see you there at five past noon! What am I? Well, you can buy me safety for $12.

Clue #2

Do you really need a book cover?
